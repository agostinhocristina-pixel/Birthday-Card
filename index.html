For You, On Your Birthday

A little something I wanted to give you from here.

I couldn't be there to hand this to you myself, so I wanted to leave a little piece of me with you instead.

A little something behind each gift

The sneakers

These are because I pay attention. Yesterday you mentioned, almost casually, that you were in need of a pair, and of course, I stored that information away. I wanted to give you something you actually needed, but also something that reminds you that I notice the little things you say.

The tulips

These are because they're beautiful, and because I wanted to give you flowers. I think a beautiful woman deserves flowers every now and then.

And, if I'm being completely honest, I remembered that little moment when I sent you pictures of tulips while I was travelling and you told me they were your favourite. I didn't forget.

And the necklace

This one is a little different.

I wanted to give you something a bit more special. Something you could keep and wear, and that might, every now and then, make you think of me.

In the end, I think these three gifts say a little something about how I feel about you:

I listen to you, I like taking care of you, and I like having little things around you that remind you of me.

I wish I could be there to see your face when you open everything.

But until then, I hope this makes the distance feel just a little bit smaller.

Happy birthday, my love.

With all my love,
Agostinho
